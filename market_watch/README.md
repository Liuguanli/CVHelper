# Market Watch

This folder stores newly discovered roles in the United States and Australia that are similar to your target positions and aligned with your current resume and research direction.

Purpose:

- Keep them separate from roles you already committed to apply for
- Manage newly discovered opportunities in one place
- Make ongoing role discovery easy to append over time
- Keep filtering grounded in your real background rather than company brand alone

Working convention:

1. Roles you have already decided to apply for go in `jobs/`.
2. Newly discovered similar roles are first recorded in `market_watch/discovered_roles.md`.
3. If you decide to prepare materials for one of those roles, create `jobs/<job-slug>/` for it.

How alerts are surfaced:

- Whenever new relevant roles are found, they should be explicitly called out in responses.
- They should also be appended to `market_watch/discovered_roles.md`.
- Roles with especially strong alignment should be marked `high-priority`.
- Each entry should include both `resume fit` and `research fit`.

What counts as a similar role:

- Backend Engineer
- Software Engineer
- Data Infrastructure / Data Engineer
- Database / Query Engine / Storage / Systems
- Applied Scientist / Research Scientist
- ML Systems / Retrieval / Data Systems roles

What counts as matching your resume and research:

- Direct mapping to your existing experience in backend systems, data infrastructure, ingestion, SQL, benchmarking, indexing, and retrieval
- Direct mapping to your PhD/Postdoc themes such as data systems, query processing, physical design, learned indexes, benchmarking, and spatial data systems
- Claims supported by your existing papers, projects, GitHub repos, or industry experience
- Gaps that can be explained honestly and reasonably, without forced fit

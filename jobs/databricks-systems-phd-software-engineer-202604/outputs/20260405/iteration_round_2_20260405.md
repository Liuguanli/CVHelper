# Iteration Round 2

## Goal

- Increase first-impression impact for HR
- Make the Databricks fit feel more immediate and memorable
- Keep the language simple, direct, and fully defensible

## What Changed

- Tightened the resume opening to a simpler and stronger systems identity
- Rewrote the cover letter opening so it immediately names the Databricks problems you match
- Simplified the first lines of the video so your role fit is obvious faster
- Refreshed ATS and HR review after the changes

## Why These Changes

- The previous round improved clarity
- This round focused on recruiter interest, not only recruiter understanding
- The strongest HR-facing improvement came from making the first impression more direct and role-specific

## What Was Intentionally Not Changed

- No new database-engine ownership claim was added
- No direct claim was added about distributed execution internals or production engine internals
- No fake scale, fake product impact, or unsupported metrics were introduced

## Outcome

- The materials now present a clearer value proposition in the first few lines
- The package is more likely to trigger recruiter interest while still staying honest
- The main remaining risk is still depth on production database engine internals, which should be handled in later technical conversations
